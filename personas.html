The proof

Tile the nine shapes, and the table fills.

Every persona overlaid on the same table. None fits inside a single column, and no cell that matters is unclaimed. That is the NaaS argument, drawn once with all nine at the same time.

Solid monograms live in a cell; outlined ones visit. The thin Transport and access row is the point, not a gap: its personas arrive with the fiber, wireless and satellite strata, and their columns are already waiting.

The org chart and the nav are the same drawing. Teams pick their layer, verbs carry their day, and the columns are where they meet.
