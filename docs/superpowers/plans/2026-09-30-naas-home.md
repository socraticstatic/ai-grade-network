# NaaS Home and Four Owner Decisions: Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:subagent-driven-development (recommended) or superpowers:executing-plans to implement this plan task-by-task. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** The NaaS rail lands on a one-screen, per-persona home that shows the whole network at a glance. Four owner decisions about the D-6 model also land.

**Architecture:**
- A `homeVals` block in naas-app.js composes fields that `vals` already computes: `observeHead`, `opsFacts`, `briefText`, `roleActAll`, `invStats`, the "new" pill, `rollup` and `problemRows`. It adds only a greeting.
- The `s0` markup shows four bands in place of the old hero.
- Decisions (a), (b), (c) and (e) are small, local changes in naas-flowmap.js, naas-verdicts.js, naas-app.js and the markup.

**Tech Stack:** dc-runtime template ("NaaS Storefront.dc.html") bound to `vals(c)`; `node --test`; headless Playwright (scripts/fold.mjs, scripts/demo-walk.mjs).

**Spec:** docs/superpowers/specs/2026-09-30-naas-home-design.md. The owner decisions are Micah's "proceed on your recommendations" (2026-09-30), restated in Tasks 3 and 4.

**Base:** branch `review-fixes` at 0651c2a (not deployed).

## Global Constraints

- 1440x900, no page scroll either way.
- No button within 10px of a card edge.
- Lists are the page, never boxed or scrolled.
- Theme tokens only; health inks come from `F.HEALTH_INK`, `F.HEALTH_WORD` and `F.healthRadius`.
- Copy: no em dashes, no codes (ER, DX, EQX, GCI), "the AT&T network", "Andi".
- New state keys go in `defaults()` and in the markup constructor (tests/review-fixes.test.mjs diffs them).
- No `sc-for` inside svg, table or select.
- Bind `disabled` (a bare attribute is dropped).
- Move the tag-census pins in tests/markup.test.mjs, with a dated comment.
- Stage by path. The commit trailer is `Co-Authored-By: Claude Opus 5.5 <noreply@anthropic.com>`.
- Gates: `npm test` bare; `node scripts/fold.mjs` (width, copy and height, both themes) at 0 FAIL; `node scripts/demo-walk.mjs` all beats.
