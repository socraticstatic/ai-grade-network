# Demo: the notes, built (2026-09-29)

Estate: **Growing** (default). Persona: **Network Eng**. 1440×900, no scrolling anywhere on the path.
Reset between runs: open the page with a fresh profile, or clear `naas.life` and `naas.tags` in local storage.

## 1. Connect is attached
Click the **Connect** tile ("5 of 7 regions"). It opens **Connect · Options**:
- Sites, data centers and cloud regions not on AT&T yet, each with a best option and what else would do.
- An IPsec branch gets AVPN, a cloud region gets NetBond.

**Proves:** the tile goes somewhere, to candidates with connectivity options.

## 2. Attach from a candidate
**Attach** on Charlotte branch drops straight into the order at the policy step, with "Attach 1 site · Charlotte branch" noted.

**Proves:** a candidate becomes an order in one click.

## 3. Observe leads with the money
The Observe head reads **"12 findings open. $41,500/mo potential savings."** Click it.
- It opens the Findings list, filtered **Open · 12 / Snoozed · 1 / Closed · 2 / All · 15**.
- Every card shows its state, age and owner.

**Proves:** the head, the list and the chips count one list.

## 4. Preview before acting
**Preview the change** on "$8,600/mo of egress rides IPsec tunnels":
- **What AT&T saw.**
- **Preview:** Today $7,100 → After $1,600, saves $5,500/mo. **Show it on the map** is one click away.
- **Evidence:** the five flow records behind it, with **Open in Logs**.
- **Timeline:** Found Aug 25 · Discovery.

Click **Acknowledge**, then the primary move. The order opens, and the finding is now In progress, owned by you, with the order on its timeline.

**Proves:** simulate, show where and how, then act. Lifecycle, owner and age.

## 5. What was actually banked
Rail **Savings** opens **Cost · Banked**:
- Banked to date $260,400.
- This month $36,000.
- Still open $41,500.
- Realised 46%.

A twelve-month running total climbs from the first attach. **Group: Business unit / Region / Cloud** splits this month's banked and still open. The rows add up to the tiles.

**Proves:** a running total of banked savings, not just potential, grouped three ways.

## 6. Access grouping and business units
- **Connect · Group: Access type** redraws the site column as AVPN, ASE on Demand, ADI, Business Fiber, AIA-B and Third Party Access. Third Party Access runs dashed through the outside lane. Open it to see its five regions, then the sites.
- **Discover · Estate · Business units:** add "Finance", then **Move to Finance** on a site. The tag survives a reload, and **Group: Business unit** on the picture and in Savings uses it.

**Proves:** access-side grouping, and customer tags by business unit.
